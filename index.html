🔻 Low Focus State

Your brain is experiencing fatigue and reduced efficiency.

⚠️ Symptoms

  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Frequent distractions
  • Slow thinking speed
  • Mental exhaustion

🛠 What to Do

  • Take a 5–10 minute break
  • Drink water and relax your eyes
  • Walk or stretch briefly
  • Switch to low-effort tasks

🚫 Avoid

  • Deep work tasks
  • Multitasking
  • Heavy screen exposure

⚡ Moderate Focus State

Your brain is stable but not at peak performance.

⚙️ Best Use

  • Routine work
  • Meetings or discussions
  • Practice tasks

💡 Tips

  • Reduce distractions
  • Use short focus intervals
  • Prepare for deep work phase

🎯 Goal

  • Transition into high focus state

🚀 High Focus State

You are in peak cognitive performance.

🔥 Best Use

  • Deep work
  • Problem solving
  • Learning complex concepts

📈 Maximize Output

  • Work without interruption
  • Silence notifications
  • Stay in flow state

⚠️ Maintain

  • Avoid context switching
  • Do not break momentum
